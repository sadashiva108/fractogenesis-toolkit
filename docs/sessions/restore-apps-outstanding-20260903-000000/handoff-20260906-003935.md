## The status and state model was rewritten

`docs/legend.md` now carries the model the owner specified on 2026-09-06, and it
is a redesign rather than two additions. **Status is carried by each finding and
the bundle's status is derived from them** — that inversion is the change
everything else follows from.

Findings: `un-started`, `framing`, `decided`, `resolved`, `reopened`,
`withdrawn`. Bundles: `un-started`, `unclaimed`, `analyzing`,
`reopened`, `resolved`, `superseded`, `withdrawn`, derived from the findings by
an ordered ladder — first row that matches wins. Sessions: `available`, `active`, `handoff`, `closed`,
`withdrawn`. `in progress` and `resolving` are gone — `framing` is the long
working status where wording and decisions are revised together, and `decided` is
the enduring fact that decisions are complete rather than a moment of activity.

**Existing bundles were deliberately not migrated**, at the owner's direction;
eleven still carry an old status and pivot when next worked. The legend carries a
transitional table mapping the old words to the new.

**What is NOT done and belongs to the successor:** the `session-management`
directory and the move of `0026`, `0027`, `0028`, `0029`, `0031`, `0032` into it;
the split of `.github/copilot-instructions.md` into session-management and
toolkit instruction sets with pointers from it; and
`docs/sessions/session-responsibilities.md`, which still describes the old
vocabulary throughout.

**`unclaimed`** is the same amount of progress as `unresolved` — none — and its
opposite on permission: closed to every session rather than open to all. The
loose end is that **`unclaimed` was retired in Revision 179** as a *session*
state, on the reasoning that nothing sits waiting to be claimed. It now returns
as a *findings bundle* status meaning something different. `0027`'s
`resolutions.md` and `docs/architecture/findings-and-sessions.md` both record the
retirement as settled. One word now names two things, one of them retired, and
`0027` is being reopened anyway.

**`reopened`** reaches only a `resolved` bundle or a `resolved` finding inside
one, and it is **the only way either may be altered.** Not a correction, not a
clarification — change the status first, and the revision becomes reviewable as
what it is.

That rule settles a tension rather than creating one. §4c says `findings.md` is
*"written once; corrected only for accuracy, never rewritten to match what was
later decided."* With `reopened` in the vocabulary, the rule is not that resolved
work is permanent but that revising it is a **declared act** — which is what §4c
was reaching for. §4c should be re-read against that; the legend does not edit it.

Ownership is independent of `reopened`: a bundle may stay with the session that
resolved it or be handed on. And it is distinct from `superseded`, which is
whole-bundle only, never a single finding, and which *preserves* the original by
creating a replacement rather than altering it in place.

**Checks that pass on things that are wrong**

  **This is this session's debt and it is unpaid.**
- `git apply` cannot unlink on the connected folder, downgrades the failure to a
  warning and exits 0, so any patch containing a deletion under-applies. Four
  instances, none committed. `0032` finding 4. It bites every `STATUS-` tag
  rename, including the four in this handoff's own revision.

**Undecided, and load-bearing**

  never says *when* it is handed over. Undecided, which is why `0029` never
  reached `resolving`.
- `0032` finding 3 asks whether a bundle whose fix is a lint belongs in the

  **Those two disagree**, and finding 3 is where it surfaces.
